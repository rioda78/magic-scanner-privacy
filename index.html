Magic Scanner – Privacy Policy / Kebijakan Privasi

Last updated / Terakhir diperbarui: 2 November 2026

Contact / Kontak: kodeigc@gmail.com

Ringkasan Bahasa Indonesia

Magic Scanner menyimpan dokumen, gambar, teks OCR, tanda tangan dan pengaturan di perangkat. Aplikasi tidak menyediakan akun dan tidak mengunggah isi dokumen ke server milik pengembang. Ekspor dan berbagi hanya dijalankan atas tindakan pengguna. SDK Google dan mesin text-to-speech dapat melakukan komunikasi jaringan sebagaimana dijelaskan di bawah. Hubungi kodeigc@gmail.com untuk pertanyaan privasi.

1. Documents and local storage

Magic Scanner processes selected photos, camera scans, imported PDFs, OCR text, document names, folders, saved signatures and app settings. These are stored in the app's private storage on your device. We do not operate a document-upload server or require an account. The local database and document files are not claimed to be independently encrypted; PDF password/security features apply only to outputs you explicitly protect.

2. Permissions and choices

Camera access is used when you choose to scan. Gallery and document imports use Android system pickers so you choose the files shared with the app. Notifications and foreground media playback support the optional Read Aloud feature. Permission denial should not prevent unrelated features from working. You can change permissions in Android Settings.

3. OCR, translation and speech

OCR, language identification and translation use Google ML Kit on the device. Required scanner components or language models may be downloaded from Google; these downloads involve network/device information handled under Google's policies. Read Aloud uses your selected device text-to-speech engine. Depending on that engine and voice, speech synthesis may use its provider's network service. Avoid using a network voice for confidential text. Recognition, summaries and translations can be inaccurate; verify important documents.

4. Advertising and third-party SDKs

Magic Scanner includes Google Mobile Ads/UMP. Ad availability depends on the build configuration and consent status. In builds where advertising is disabled, the app does not initialize ad requests or display ads through its ad controller. When enabled, the Google advertising SDK may collect/share IP-derived approximate location, advertising/device or account identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ad-related transfers use Google's SDK safeguards, including transport encryption. Available consent/privacy options appear in About when required by UMP. Document images, OCR content, signatures and PDF passwords are not supplied as advertising targeting data by our app code.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Mobile Ads disclosures: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

4b. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

Release builds use Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, app version and an installation identifier. Crash reports never include your documents, scans or OCR text. They are used only to fix bugs. / Versi rilis memakai Firebase Crashlytics untuk laporan crash (jejak error, model perangkat, versi OS dan aplikasi, ID instalasi), tanpa isi dokumen atau hasil scan.

5. Export and sharing

You choose whether to export, print, copy, read aloud or share a document. A selected destination, cloud-storage provider, printer service, speech engine or receiving app handles the data under its own policies. Those actions can move information off your device. Files exported or shared outside the app remain under the control of their destination and are not removed when you delete the original in Magic Scanner.

6. Retention and deletion

Documents stay on the device until deleted. Moving a document to Trash allows recovery before permanent deletion. Trash entries older than the default 30-day retention may be permanently removed when cleanup runs; you can also permanently delete them yourself. App cache/import/export temporary files may persist until cleanup or app-data removal. To remove all app-local records, use Android's Clear storage or uninstall. This does not remove exported copies from other apps or services.

Android cloud backup and app-data transfer are disabled/excluded by this app's configuration to protect document contents. Device-maker behavior can vary; do not treat this as an independent encryption guarantee. Keep your own copies of documents you need before uninstalling or clearing storage.

7. Children and sensitive documents

The app is a general-purpose document utility, not designed specifically for children. Users should only process documents they have permission to use. We do not ask users to submit identity documents or financial/health records to the developer. Review the selected sharing destination carefully for sensitive documents.

8. Questions and updates

For support, privacy questions or requests concerning information you have voluntarily emailed us, contact kodeigc@gmail.com. Support messages are used to answer the request and may be retained as needed to resolve it; do not send document contents unless necessary. We may update this policy when the app's behavior changes and will update the date above.
